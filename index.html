Rethinking prescription, beyond the brief

Cegedim · 2025

June 2025. A restyle of the prescription section reached me already decided.

Same structure. Same lack of vertical space.

I built a proof of concept in Figma. The brief needed a third library to maintain.

First, the architect.

It doesn't fix the vertical space, and it needs a third library.

STOP!

Then the PM. The original plan was dropped.

The reframe: search and the ongoing prescription always in view, one click away.

Beyond the brief: the settings of eight sections in one dashboard. Nobody asked for it.

Shipped.

Both, in the same release.
