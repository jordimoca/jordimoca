About me

I design products that make hard workflows feel obvious.

Ten years designing digital products for people who depend on them to do their job — the professional platform doctors, physiotherapists and clinic staff use to run their practice, digital banking for international financial institutions, and high-traffic media. My practice sits at the intersection of systems architecture, interaction design, and deep domain knowledge.

I approach every problem as a systems challenge first. Before any wireframe, I map information flows, identify decision points, and understand the mental model of the person doing the work. Then I design the simplest interface that fits that model.

I work hand-in-hand with Product rather than waiting on finished specs, and I challenge a brief when the evidence calls for it. When the prescription section redesign reached me already defined, I built a proof of concept showing it was unfeasible, defended it with the software architect and took it to the Product Manager. The original approach was dropped and the reframing I proposed shipped instead.
